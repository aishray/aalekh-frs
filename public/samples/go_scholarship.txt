GOVERNMENT OF RAJYAPRADESH
SOCIAL WELFARE DEPARTMENT

G.O. No. SW/2026/SCH/118                                   Dated: 14 August 2026

Subject: Online processing of the State Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme through a web portal and Common Service Centres.

1. Background
The State Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme is presently administered through paper applications submitted at institutions and forwarded to District Social Welfare Offices. Applications take 6 to 9 months to be sanctioned, files are lost in transit, and students have no means of knowing the status of their application.

2. Objective
Government has decided that all applications under the scheme, fresh and renewal, shall be received, verified, sanctioned and paid online from the academic year 2026-27, so that eligible students receive the scholarship in a transparent and time-bound manner.

3. Eligibility
3.1 The applicant shall be a domicile of Rajyapradesh.
3.2 The annual income of the applicant's family from all sources shall not exceed Rs. 2,50,000 (Rupees two lakh fifty thousand only).
3.3 The applicant shall be studying in a post-matric course in an institution recognised by the competent authority in Rajyapradesh.
3.4 For renewal, the applicant shall have attended at least 75% of classes in the previous academic year, as certified by the institution.

4. Application
4.1 Applications shall be submitted online through the scholarship portal or through Common Service Centres (CSCs).
4.2 Aadhaar-based e-KYC of the applicant shall be mandatory.
4.3 Caste and income certificates shall be obtained from DigiLocker where available; otherwise the applicant shall upload the certificate in the prescribed format in Annexure A.

5. Verification
5.1 The nodal officer of the institution shall verify the application within 15 days of submission.
5.2 The District Social Welfare Officer shall sanction or reject the verified application within 15 days of receipt.
5.3 Reasons shall be recorded in writing for every rejection and communicated to the applicant.

6. Disbursement
6.1 The scholarship shall be paid through Direct Benefit Transfer using PFMS to the Aadhaar-seeded bank account of the applicant.
6.2 The scholarship shall be paid in a single instalment not later than 31 March of the academic year.

7. Grievance
7.1 An online grievance facility shall be provided to applicants.
7.2 The applicant shall be informed by SMS at every stage of processing of the application.
7.3 Grievances shall be resolved within 7 working days of receipt.

8. Monitoring
8.1 A dashboard shall be provided to the District Social Welfare Officer showing district-wise status of applications.
8.2 A monthly report shall be submitted to the Principal Secretary, Social Welfare Department.

9. Service timeline
The scholarship shall be sanctioned within 30 working days of submission of a complete application, and the service is notified under the Rajyapradesh Right to Service Act.

10. Data protection
Personal data collected under the scheme shall be used only for the purposes of the scheme.

By order and in the name of the Governor of Rajyapradesh
