GOVERNMENT OF RAJYAPRADESH
SOCIAL WELFARE DEPARTMENT

Corrigendum No. SW/2026/SCH/118-A                           Dated: 2 September 2026

Subject: Corrigendum to G.O. No. SW/2026/SCH/118 dated 14 August 2026.

1. In paragraph 3.2 of the G.O. read above, for "Rs. 2,50,000 (Rupees two lakh fifty thousand only)" read "Rs. 3,00,000 (Rupees three lakh only)". The annual family income limit for eligibility is accordingly Rs. 3,00,000 per annum.
2. All other conditions of the G.O. remain unchanged.
